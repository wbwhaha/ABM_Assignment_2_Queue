## ABM_Assignment_2_Queue

This agent-based model simulates human queueing behavior in structured environments, examining interactions between different personality types—patient, assertive, and rule-breaker. It explores how these traits influence queue dynamics, waiting times, emotional responses, and service efficiency. Key research areas include the effects of queue-jumping, frustration-induced abandonment, and system resilience to assertive and disruptive behaviors, with a focus on emergent queue structures and emotional stress patterns.
